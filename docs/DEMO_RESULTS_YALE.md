Best fix: fit the windows on bed2-e with laminated acoustic glass (OITC 35) and new seals, -7.8 dB. Window replacements also reset the leak fraction to 0.001 (SOUND_MODEL.md 3.6, placeholder); laminated OITC 35 is a centre-of-glass rating, so a whole unit on site can be up to 6 dB worse (SOUND_MODEL.md 6.B7).
Not applicable here: seal only (the frames already have the sealed leak fraction); close an interior door (none is open).
