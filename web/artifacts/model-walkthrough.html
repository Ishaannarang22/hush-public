What is measured, assumed, and still missing?

Measured geometry: Matterport room outlines and 29 walls, with a 1.60 × 1.59 m bedroom window and a 2.40 × 2.02 m sliding glass door from the survey. Window-width edits are hypothetical; original dimensions restore with Reset.

Assumed: surface absorption and transmission spectra, source frequency shapes, opening states, and equivalent point-source placement. The traffic example uses the saved emissions estimate, not a live feed. The five-source example is deliberately illustrative. Google is unconnected.

Two related estimates: the room view solves steady-state energy exchange between rooms; the path view computes a listening-height slice at 1.2 m using direct transmission, first reflection, and single-edge diffraction. The floor colors are noise levels, not moving particles or a pressure-wave animation.

Coverage: aircraft affect the room estimate in the five-source scenario, but are excluded from the spatial grid. Roof paths, flanking transmission, seal leakage, higher reflections, and wave interference are not included. Only the two survey-identified exposed walls inject outdoor energy into the room balance. Other apartment boundaries do not inject neighboring-unit noise.

Offline artifact: outdoor wall levels were freshly computed when this file was built. The controls recalculate the room and path solvers in your browser. New road counts or flight tracks require rerunning the full pipeline. The earlier field test did not validate this combined model.
